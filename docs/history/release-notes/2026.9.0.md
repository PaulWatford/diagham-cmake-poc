# Release 2026.9.0 — DiagHam r4493 with a CMake build, a physics test suite and a documentation set

Purpose: the text of the GitHub Release for 2026.9.0, kept here so the site and the repository carry it.
Source: `CHANGELOG.md` and the r4493 build of 2026-09-28; numbers from `docs/reference/test-coverage.md`.

## What this is

The DiagHam exact-diagonalization toolkit (Nicolas Regnault, Gunnar Möller and collaborators; Subversion trunk **r4493**, 2026-09-18, full history on the `upstream` branch with authors by name) built by CMake instead of autotools, with:

- **61 libraries and 603 programs** building on Linux with GCC and Clang, with or without LAPACK, GSL, GMP, FFTW3, bzip2, MPI and ScaLAPACK (presets `default`, `lapack`, `full`, `hpc`, `core`, `mkl`), installable and usable from another project through `find_package(DiagHam)`;
- a **ctest suite of 738 tests**: 107 physics goldens whose answers are known independently of DiagHam (exact counting of Hilbert-space dimensions and of Laughlin, Moore–Read and Read–Rezayi zero modes on the sphere, torus, cylinder and disk; Coulomb pseudopotentials from Wigner algebra; Jack polynomials against exact diagonalisation; entanglement counting; free-fermion, AKLT and Haldane–Shastry chains; Hubbard tight binding; independent numpy diagonalisations of spin chains and of pseudopotential Hamiltonians), 4 cross-checks between algorithms, 12 regression spectra, 14 self-tests of the harness, 2 known-bug reproducers, and a `--help` smoke test of every program. **34 of 603 programs have their physics checked, 11 more have a regression test; 555 are smoke-tested only** — `docs/reference/test-coverage.md` is the honest list;
- a **documentation set** (tutorials, how-to guides, reference, explanation; the wiki's 132 program manuals attached to a generated `--help` reference of every program) published at <https://paulwatford.github.io/diagham-cmake-poc/>;
- **14 fixes to DiagHam sources**, each an ordinary commit with its root cause, indexed in `docs/explanation/upstream-fixes.md`; **31 registered defects** in `docs/reference/known-defects.md`, four found by this test suite (U28 fixed: cylinder pseudopotentials crashed without GSL; U29: a disk program hangs; U30: plain Lanczos can return an energy below the ground state on a degenerate spectrum; U31: the two-band checkerboard model segfaults);
- reproducibility: every build can record its environment (`scripts_cmake/environment.sh`), CI keeps that record, and a Docker recipe builds and tests the whole thing;
- a documented, tested procedure to bring new Subversion revisions in (`scripts_cmake/sync_upstream.sh`), a weekly drift check, and governance that leaves the physics with the DiagHam authors.

## What changed for users of the autotools build

`cmake --preset default && cmake --build --preset default && ctest --preset default` replaces `./bootstrap && ./configure --enable-… && make`; `docs/reference/configure-flag-map.md` maps every `configure` flag to its CMake option. Two FQHEOnDisk programs were renamed because their names collided with FQHEOnSphere ones at install: `FQHEDiskBosonsDelta`, `FQHEDiskLaughlinMonteCarloOverlap`. Results are written at full double precision (17 significant digits) instead of 14.

## Known limitations

- Most programs are smoke-tested only (see above); a green suite says the checked ones are right, not that all are.
- `FQHEDiskFermionsTwoBodyGeneric` hangs (U29); plain Lanczos with `-n 1` is unreliable on degenerate spectra (U30) — use `-n 4` or `--force-reorthogonalize`; `FCICheckerboardLatticeModel` needs `--single-band` (U31).
- The Kent cluster recipe and the Apptainer image are untested drafts.
