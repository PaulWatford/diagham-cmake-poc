[FQHETorusBosonsEntanglementEntropyParticlePartition](FQHETorusBosonsEntanglementEntropyParticlePartition.md) allows to compute the entanglement entropy and the entanglement spectrum for bosons on the torus geometry. The system is cut in particle space. Usage is similar to [FQHESphereBosonEntanglementEntropy](FQHESphereBosonEntanglementEntropy.md) or [FQHETopInsulatorEntanglementEntropyParticlePartition](FTIEntanglementEntropyParticlePartition.md). Notice that the states have to be provided without the Kx symmetry.
