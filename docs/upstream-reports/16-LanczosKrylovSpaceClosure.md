# 16. Plain Lanczos: detect the closure of the Krylov space instead of iterating on round-off (U30)

Source: commit `968f78018` on `main` (generated by `scripts_cmake/upstream_pack.py`, do not edit; edit the commit message or the fix index instead).

**Base**: SVN r4493. **Files**: `src/LanczosAlgorithm/BasicLanczosAlgorithm.cc`, `src/LanczosAlgorithm/BasicLanczosAlgorithm.h`, `src/LanczosAlgorithm/ComplexBasicLanczosAlgorithm.cc`, `src/LanczosAlgorithm/ComplexBasicLanczosAlgorithm.h`.

## What, why, how it was verified

BasicLanczosAlgorithm and ComplexBasicLanczosAlgorithm (the classes behind
-n 1 without --force-reorthogonalize) normalised the residual of the
three-term recurrence without looking at its size. When the start vector
spans an invariant subspace -- which happens after a handful of steps on a
degenerate spectrum -- that residual is round-off (about 1e-14 here); the
normalised noise became the next Lanczos vector and the tridiagonal matrix
filled with garbage. Ritz values then fall outside the spectrum:
HubbardSquareLatticeModel -p 8 -x 2 -y 4 --u-potential 0 -n 1 --full-diag 100
reported -18.412 and -17.868 in two momentum sectors, below the exact
ground energy -16 (defect U30, docs/reference/known-defects.md).

Traced on that case: residual norms of order 10 for steps 2 to 8, then
2.3e-14 at step 9 (the sector has 10 distinct levels), then order 10
again -- pure noise -- for the remaining 69 iterations until the spurious
value "converged".

Fix: after forming the residual, compare its norm with 1e-13 times the
scale of the recurrence (the largest of the last two diagonal and the last
off-diagonal elements). Below that the Krylov space has closed: the
tridiagonal matrix is truncated to the closed dimension, its eigenvalues
are exact, a message says so and the iteration stops; later calls report
the result as converged. If fewer exact eigenvalues exist than were
requested, a warning is printed and the old behaviour is kept. The
non-degenerate U = 4 case is untouched (no closure; plain Lanczos agrees
with full diagonalisation to 1e-13, as before). The same pattern exists
in the eleven sibling classes (WithGroundState, DiskStorage, Projector
variants); they are not changed here, this fix covers the reported path.

All eight momentum sectors of the U = 0 case now give the tight-binding
minimum of the sector (-16, -14, -12, ...). The known-bug test
knownbug.hubbard.2x4.U0.plain_lanczos_below_ground_state is promoted to
physics.hubbard.2x4.U0.plain_lanczos_krylov_closure: it compares every
sector's energy with tests/oracles/spin_hubbard.py (new
tight_binding_square_sectors: the lowest filling of each total-momentum
sector, tests/data/spin_hubbard/hubbard_2x4_N8_U0_sectors.dat).

docs/reference/known-defects.md: U30 marked fixed. docs/upstream-reports/:
open-U30.md removed, report and Subversion-applicable diff 16 generated by
